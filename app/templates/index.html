For marketing partners

Partners help PickBook grow by sharing the app and bringing paying readers through a unique referral code.

  • Direct referrals: 20% recurring commission from each active premium subscriber who uses your code.
  • Marketing pool: 15% of organic premium subscription revenue may be shared among active partners.
  • Free signups do not count for payout. Payouts are based on successfully processed premium subscriptions.
  • The referral system is one level only. There are no sub-commissions from people referred by your referrals.

For micro-investors

Investors support infrastructure and operations. This is a revenue-sharing deal, not ownership or equity in PickBook.

  • Revenue-share option: receive 15% of premium subscription revenue until the agreed cap is reached.
  • Subscriber-slots option: receive the value of agreed active premium subscriber slots when paying subscribers exist.
  • The default sample agreement uses a 2x cap: for example, ₦15,000 can cap at ₦30,000.
  • Investor payment starts through Paystack, but the signed agreement should also be uploaded for records.
